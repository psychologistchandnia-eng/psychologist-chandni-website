## 8. Filename and alt-text suggestions

Use plain descriptive filenames and factual alt text on the website. Examples:

- `chandni-akhenia.jpg` — “Chandni Akhenia, psychologist in Malad West, Mumbai.”
- `sun-multispeciality-hospital-entrance.jpg` — “Entrance to Sun Multispeciality Hospital on BJ Patel Road, Malad West.”
- `consultation-room-malad-west.jpg` — “Private consultation room at the Malad West appointment location.”

Only use the latter two if those exact, permitted photos are provided. Keep decorative line art on the website marked decorative with empty alt text.

## 9. Review-request strategy

Offer every client the same optional, neutral invitation to leave an honest review. Do not offer incentives, request only positive reviews, pressure clients, or make care conditional on a review. Do not ask clients to disclose a diagnosis or treatment details. When replying publicly, thank the reviewer without confirming that they are a client or discussing care.
